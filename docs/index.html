Privacy Policy for Germanium

Last updated: August 2026

Germanium is a German-language learning app. This Privacy Policy explains what user data the app collects, processes, or shares.

Data Collection

Germanium does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app functions entirely offline and does not connect to any external server, analytics service, or third party.

Data Storage

Germanium tracks which sentences you get wrong, so it can repeat those more often to help you learn. This mistake-tracking data, along with your general progress and exercise history, is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone, including the developer.

Third-Party Services

Germanium does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

Children's Privacy

Since Germanium collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if a feature requiring data collection is added), this page will be updated accordingly.
